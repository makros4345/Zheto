Our Story

Golden Crust started with one oven and a family sourdough recipe. Fifteen years later we still bake every loaf by hand, starting at 4 a.m., so the shelves are full when you walk in.

We use local flour, real butter and no shortcuts. Thank you for keeping a neighborhood bakery alive.
