# Websites for Local Businesses: Starter Playbook

## Step 1: Build a portfolio (this week)
- Make 3 demo sites for fictional businesses in different niches (bakery is done; next try a barber or salon, and a plumber or cleaner).
- Put them online for free on Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop), GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages.
- Make one simple page for yourself that shows the demos, your prices and how to contact you.

## Step 2: Find clients
Look for businesses that have **no website**, a **broken or outdated site**, or **only a Facebook page**:
- Search Google Maps for "[business type] near [your town]" and note businesses with no website link.
- Start with: restaurants, barbers, salons, cleaners, plumbers, electricians, auto shops, tutors and churches.
- Keep a list in a spreadsheet: name, phone, what's missing, date contacted and result.

## Step 3: Reach out
- **Best approach:** build a free 1-page preview for them (about 30 minutes with Claude), then show it to them.
  People say yes much more often when they can see their own site.
- Walk in, send a direct message or email. Keep it short:

> Hi [Name], I noticed [Business] doesn't have a website, so customers searching on Google can't
> easily find your hours or menu. I made a quick free preview of what yours could look like: [link].
> If you like it, I can make it live this week. Want to take a look?

## Step 4: Pricing (adjust to your country)
| Package | What's included | Price idea |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1-page site, mobile friendly, contact/call button, Google Maps | $150–$400 |
| Standard | 3–5 pages, contact form, basic SEO, Google Business profile setup | $400–$1,000 |
| Care plan | Hosting, updates, small edits each month | $20–$75/month |

The care plan is how you build monthly income: 20 clients at $40 a month comes to $800 a month.

## Step 5: Deliver
1. Collect their logo, photos, menu or services, hours, address and phone number.
2. Build the site with Claude and send it for review (allow 1–2 rounds of changes).
3. Buy their domain (about $10–15/year; let the client own it) and connect it to free hosting.
4. Set up or improve their Google Business Profile.
5. Get paid (take 50% up front), then ask for a review and referrals.
